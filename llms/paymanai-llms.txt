## What We Do

Payman AI enables banks and credit unions to deploy AI agents that can execute real financial transactions - payments, transfers, account analysis - through voice or text, on existing banking rails. Every action is policy-validated, logged, and auditable.

## Architecture

- **AI Reasoning Layer**: Understands customer intent from natural language
- **Policy Enforcement Layer**: Validates every action against bank-defined rules
- **Deterministic Execution Layer**: Executes transactions through existing core banking systems

## Integrations

- Core Banking: FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry, COCC, and more
- Digital Platforms: Narmi, Q2, Alkami, Candescent, and more
- Channels: Voice, Mobile, Web, and SMS

## FAQ

- **What is Payman AI?** Payman AI deploys AI agents for banks and credit unions that carry out real banking transactions on the institution's existing systems under policies, approvals and audit trails the institution controls.
- **Who is Payman AI for?** Banks and credit unions, their customers and members, and their staff.
- **Is it safe / how do bank policies control agents?** The agent acts only when an authenticated customer or member asks; an immutable policy engine checks every action, every step is logged, and the institution holds the kill switch.
- **Is Payman SOC 2 compliant?** Yes. Payman has SOC 2 Type I and SOC 2 Type II reports. Details: https://paymanai.com/security and https://trust.paymanai.com/
- **Does Payman replace the core?** No. It runs on the institution's existing rails and integrates with existing cores and digital platforms rather than replacing them.
- **How is Payman funded?** Payman AI has raised $13M in total funding.
